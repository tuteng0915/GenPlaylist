# Participant Information and Consent

**Study:** History-Conditioned Personalized Music Generation  
**Institution:** {{INSTITUTION}}  
**Principal investigator:** {{PI_NAME_AND_CONTACT}}  
**Ethics protocol or exemption:** {{APPROVAL_IDENTIFIER}}

You are invited to take part in a research study about how people perceive
music generated from listening-history examples. Participation is voluntary.
The task takes approximately {{DURATION_MINUTES}} minutes. You will inspect one
listening-history context, hear two 30-second candidate clips, rate both clips,
and state which you prefer. One candidate is machine-generated and the other is
a real held-out track; their order is hidden during the task.

We collect your ratings, listening frequency, whether you have formal musical
training, an optional comment, and a pseudonymous recruitment code. We do not
store the raw recruitment code. Optional comments may inadvertently contain
identifying information, so please do not include names or contact details.
Your data will be retained for {{RETENTION_PERIOD}} and accessed only by
{{AUTHORIZED_TEAM}}. The research results may be published in aggregate form.

Possible risks include fatigue, discomfort with a song or lyric, and ordinary
privacy risks associated with online research. You may stop before submission
without penalty. For questions about the study or withdrawal of a submitted
response, contact {{STUDY_CONTACT}} and provide your recruitment code. For
questions about participant rights, contact {{ETHICS_CONTACT}}.

Compensation is {{COMPENSATION}}. By selecting the consent checkbox, you confirm
that you are at least {{MINIMUM_AGE}}, have read this statement, and voluntarily
agree to participate.
